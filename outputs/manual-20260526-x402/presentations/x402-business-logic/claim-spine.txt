Thesis:
Netstars can turn x402 from a technical protocol demo into a merchant-facing Agent Commerce payment and AI Token operating service.

Audience:
Netstars / Wea Japan operating and commercial decision makers.

One-line arc:
The market is shifting toward agent-initiated micro-transactions; Netstars has an unusual fit because it owns both payment aggregation and AI Token sales; the demo must therefore prove a sellable closed loop, then move through focused GTM and ecosystem expansion.

Slides:
1. x402 should be positioned as a commercial infrastructure opportunity, not a protocol experiment.
2. Agent Commerce creates a new payment job: autonomous, small-value, high-frequency, instantly auditable transactions.
3. Netstars' current assets match the demand pattern better than a pure PSP, wallet, or AI reseller.
4. The winning product is a four-layer service package that hides chain complexity from merchants.
5. The demo must prove a revenue chain: USDC payment -> Token credit -> AI usage -> billing and settlement.
6. Role boundaries make the offer credible: Netstars owns the customer surface, Wea executes chain operations.
7. Token operations are the commercial center of gravity, because they create recurring usage, billing, and account data.
8. Trust architecture is a buying criterion, not a back-office topic.
9. GTM should begin with a narrow wedge where Token consumption and payment pain are already visible.
10. Ecosystem expansion should connect AI providers, Agent platforms, merchants, and settlement partners around Netstars.
11. Product roadmap should separate demo proof, pilot hardening, commercialization, and platform scaling.
12. Management should approve scope, operating model, and pilot packaging.
